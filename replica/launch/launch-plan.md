# Launch plan

## Gate: don't announce until these are true

1. ~~The four cheap fixes~~ Built 2026-10-13: answer change, timer off or extra time, one settings panel, rename,
   duplicate and drag to reorder. Still missing from that list: extra time per student (it is per game).
2. A privacy policy and terms, written for schools. Students are often under 13 or under 16. The app stores a
   display name, answers and score only, which helps, but COPPA, FERPA and GDPR questions are yours to settle with
   a lawyer before schools use it. No policy page exists yet.
3. A support address on the site. None exists.
4. `helmroom.app` bought, the trademark rows in `brand.md` run, and the GitHub repo renamed.
5. Password reset by email. Teachers who forget a password are locked out today.
6. Mobile layout checked at 390px, and browser tests (Playwright) for the live game.
7. Supabase on a paid plan, or accept that the free project pauses when idle and the game goes down with it.

## Before launch

- A waitlist or beta list. There is no signup capture except accounts themselves.
- Analytics (page views, signups, games started) and error tracking. Neither is installed.
- 10 teachers you can reach by hand. Run a real game with each, watch where they get stuck.

## Where to find the original's unhappy users

I could not reach Reddit or Capterra in this session, so I have no verified threads. Places to search yourself,
reading rather than scraping: r/Teachers and r/edtech for the original's name plus "alternative" or "switched",
teacher Facebook groups, and the Capterra and TrustRadius review pages. Start with the 1 to 3 star reviews.

## The post

Lead with the fix, not the clone: what a teacher can now do that took a hunt through menus. Product Hunt or Hacker
News "Show HN", only once the gate above is done. Don't name the original in the title. Don't use reviewers' words.

## First 10 users

Teachers from your own network or school, one live game each, then ask: what did you look for and not find?
Write the answers into `fixes.md` and re-rank.
